1The cut · engine/PuzzleCut.kt

Every edge is cut once and shared by two pieces.

Each inner edge is ten points and three Bézier curves, with a random tab direction and a little jitter. The piece on one side walks it forwards, the piece on the other side walks it backwards, so the two always fit.

Knobs in units of the cell

Offsets are multiples of unit = min(cellW, cellH), so knobs stay round on cells that are not square. TAB = 0.1 sets the knob size and JITTER = 0.035 moves five of the points so no two pieces look the same.

pointalong ÷ lengthoffset ÷ unit

One seed rebuilds the whole puzzle

The edges come from Random(seed). A saved game stores the grid, the seed and where each piece lies, never a bitmap, and the same shapes come back on resume. Each piece is rendered once into a bitmap padded by 0.355 × unit, the furthest a control point can reach.

// (distance along the edge, offset from the edge) for p0..p9, from PuzzleCut.edge()
val pts = floatArrayOf(
    0f, 0f,
    0.2f * length, a * u,
    half + (b + d) * u, (-t + c) * u,
    half + (-t + b) * u, (t + c) * u,
    half + (-2 * t + b - d) * u, (3 * t + c) * u,
    half + (2 * t + b - d) * u, (3 * t + c) * u,
    half + (t + b) * u, (t + c) * u,
    half + (b + d) * u, (-t + c) * u,
    0.8f * length, e * u,
    length, 0f,
)
2On the table · ui/game

Pieces join on the table, like a real jigsaw.

Drop a piece next to its neighbour and the two move as one group from then on. Drop a group near its place in the frame and the whole group locks in. The table tools are there for the big puzzles.

Try the tray sort on the board's pieces.

These are the pieces of the puzzle on the board at the top, rendered by the same port. The sort keys are the app's: edgeRank for edges first, and for color, the hue of each piece's average color, with greys after the colors from light to dark.

Hint

The lightbulb outlines one piece that is not placed yet, and the spot where it goes, in a pulsing yellow.

GameViewModel.showHint

Tray sorting

Edges first (corners, then border pieces, then the middle), by color around the color wheel, or shuffled.

Picture help

A slider for how strongly the picture shows under the pieces (30% by default), and a preview card you can drag anywhere and tap to enlarge.

Zoom and pan

Pinch to zoom from half the fitted size up to 8 times, drag the empty table to pan. An edge-pieces filter hides the middle pieces.

Sounds and haptics

A snap, a join and a win sound, synthesized by tools/sounds.py, a haptic on every snap and confetti at the end.

Games save as you play and resume where you left off. Best times are kept per puzzle and per piece count.

3Kids tab · 37 puzzles

Big pieces that click in before you let go.

In Kids mode a piece drops itself as soon as it is close to its spot, so a child does not have to let go at exactly the right place. Every piece's outline is drawn on the board, and a missed piece goes back to the tray instead of getting lost on the table.

Kids lock

The child icon on the home screen shows only Kids puzzles. To unlock, a grown-up holds the button for 3 seconds. Try it.

Locked

The picture's word, in English or French.

The app reads the word with the phone's text-to-speech, in French when the phone is set to French and a French voice is installed, at speech rate 0.9. Here your browser's voice reads the same strings from catalog.json.

All Kids illustrations are drawn by tools/kids_illustrations.py (Python and Pillow, 1600 × 1200, with the Fredoka font). Counting cards go from 1 to 10; letter cards use words that start with the same letter in English and French.

4Your photos · ui/crop

Any photo becomes a puzzle, and stays on the phone.

The app has no internet permission, so photos never leave the device. It uses the system Photo Picker and the camera app, which need no storage or camera permission of their own.

Pick or shoot

"From photo" opens the gallery through the Photo Picker, or the camera through a FileProvider.

Crop

Keep the original shape or crop to 1:1, 4:3 or 3:4, framing the picture with your fingers.

Keep

The photo is copied into the app's own storage, downscaled to 1600 px on the long side with EXIF rotation applied.

Play

It appears under "My photos". The grid is chosen to keep pieces close to square for that photo's shape.

The demo at the top does the same in your browser: choose Your photo and the picture is cut locally, without being uploaded.

5Inside · app/src/main

One activity, a pure Kotlin engine, and JSON files.

No backend, no database and no DI framework. The engine has no Android dependency except the renderer, so the cutting and the grid are covered by plain JUnit tests.

7Install

Install the APK, or build it yourself.

Puzzles is not in an app store. Download the APK from GitHub on the phone, or build it from source with Gradle.

On the phone

  1. Open this page on the Android phone or tablet (Android 8.0 or newer).
  2. Tap Download APK below and open the downloaded file.
  3. When Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.
  4. Updating later: install the new APK over the old one. Saved games are kept.
puzzles-1.2.0.apk 9.0 MBAll releases

From source

Needs the Android SDK (compile SDK 36) and JDK 17 or newer.

git clone https://github.com/ouaazizdriss/puzzles
cd puzzles
./gradlew assembleDebug      # app/build/outputs/apk/debug/app-debug.apk
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest  # engine unit tests

Release builds are signed when a keystore.properties file, not committed, points to a keystore. tools/kids_illustrations.py redraws the Kids pictures and tools/sounds.py the sound effects.
